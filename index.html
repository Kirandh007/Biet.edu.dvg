Bapuji Educational Association

Empowering generations through 60+ institutions in education since 1958.

With a legacy spanning over six decades, Bapuji Educational Association stands as a beacon of holistic education in Karnataka. It runs more than 60 institutions — nursery through postgraduate, spanning engineering, medicine, dentistry, pharmacy and nursing.

That breadth is what earned Davanagere its name as the Oxford of Karnataka. BIET has been the association's engineering college since 1979.

Our charter

Vision & mission

Our vision
To be a nationally recognized technical institution that empowers engineers to create sustainable, technology-driven solutions through excellence in education, research, and effective leadership.

Our mission

  • 01To develop competent, ethically grounded, socially responsible engineers through outcome-based and experiential learning.
  • 02To foster advanced research, innovation, and emerging technologies that generate sustainable solutions for societal and industrial challenges.
  • 03To nurture entrepreneurial mindsets, social innovators, and visionary leaders capable of creating impact on industry, startups, and society.
  • 04To establish an industry-integrated, globally connected learning ecosystem for continuous professional development and lifelong learning.

Leadership

Messages from the leadership.

Chairman's message

"We measure our success by the engineers we send into the world."

Since the Bapuji Educational Association was founded in 1958, our guiding belief has been simple: that quality education is the surest path to opportunity, and that it must remain within reach of every deserving student. BIET carries that conviction forward — pairing academic rigor with the values, discipline and sense of service that define a complete engineer.

To our students, faculty and partners — thank you for building this institution with us. The years ahead will ask more of all of us, and I have no doubt BIET will rise to meet them.

Sri. S.S. Mallikarjun
Chairman
Principal's message

"Discipline in the classroom, and character outside it."

BIET's autonomy is a responsibility as much as a privilege — it lets us shape curricula that move as fast as industry does, while holding firmly to the fundamentals that make an engineer dependable. My door stays open to every student who wants to build something worth building.

Arvind
Principal
Director's message

"An institution is only as strong as the research it backs."

Our fifteen recognised research centres exist so that curiosity has somewhere to go after the classroom. Whether it's a first-year student's first hackathon or a doctoral candidate's fifth year, BIET's job is to keep giving that curiosity room to grow.

Prof. Y Vrushabhendrappa
Director
